**Last updated:** March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **My Bar: Home Cocktails** ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the mobile application.

This policy is intended to support app distribution requirements for Apple App Store and Google Play.

## 1. Who We Are

Product name: **My Bar: Home Cocktails**  
Developer website: [https://logion-web.ru/](https://logion-web.ru/)

If you have privacy questions, you can contact us through the developer website above.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the My Bar: Home Cocktails mobile application and its in-app features.

## 3. Information We Process

### 3.1 Information you create in the App

The App lets you create and manage your own bar catalog, including:

- ingredient and cocktail entries,
- tags, notes, recipe data, and measurements,
- image URLs or local file paths you provide.

This information is stored locally on your device.

### 3.2 Files you choose to import/export/share

When you use import/export/share features, the App processes files you explicitly select (for example, JSON catalog files) in order to complete your action.

### 3.3 Limited device/app data used for app functions

The App may access:

- battery state (to support optional power-saving behavior),
- app version/build information (for in-app "About" details),
- photo library access (only when you choose to pick an image).

### 3.4 Information we do not collect for profiling

We do **not** use the App for:

- account registration,
- behavioral advertising,
- user profiling,
- selling personal data.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information only to provide App functionality, including:

- displaying and editing your bar data,
- saving your preferences on your device,
- importing/exporting/sharing catalog files when requested by you,
- loading remote images only when you provide a URL.

## 5. Legal Basis (where applicable)

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, we rely on:

- **performance of a service** you request (core app features),
- **legitimate interests** (app reliability and basic maintenance),
- **consent/device permission** where platform permissions are required (for example, photo library picker).

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data.

Data may be disclosed only in the following cases:

- when you explicitly use system share features,
- when required by law or valid legal process,
- to protect rights, safety, and security.

## 7. Third-Party Services and Platform Components

The App uses platform and open-source components (for example, iOS/Android system APIs and Flutter ecosystem packages) to provide file selection, sharing, and local storage features.

These components may process data necessary to perform the requested operation on your device.

## 8. Data Storage and Retention

- App data is primarily stored locally on your device.
- We do not operate a user account backend for this App.
- You can delete your data by:
  - deleting entries in the App,
  - clearing app storage via device settings,
  - uninstalling the App.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Because data is primarily stored locally, routine international transfer by us is limited. If you share data or open external links, transfer rules depend on your actions and the selected service.

## 10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate for a local-first mobile app. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through account-based services.

## 12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, or portability regarding personal data.

Because this App is local-first, most controls are available directly on your device and in the App UI.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date.
